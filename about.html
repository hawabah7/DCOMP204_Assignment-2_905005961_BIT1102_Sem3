Our Mission

Born on Mount Aureol, Built for Freetown

CampusNest was founded by tertiary institution students who experienced firsthand the difficulty of finding reliable housing. We replace standard house hunting stresses with transparent, pre-vetted options to protect students from unfair pricing or unexpected security issues.

Our 3-Step Verification Standard

01

Document Check

We review property deeds, utility tracking history, and ownership credentials before any room can be listed on our grid.

02

Property Inspection

Our field support agents physically inspect structural safety, locking assemblies, running water parameters, and electrical items.

03

Verified Listing

Only rooms passing all compliance parameters receive our verified badge status badge on the public feed system.

Essential Student Safety Tips

Always Inspect First

Never send deposits via Orange Money or Africell Money before a physical viewing or verification check.

Check Utilities & Access

Confirm the water storage infrastructure limits and verify safety during late evening return routes.

Sign Written Agreements

Ensure any agreed maintenance terms or tenancy constraints are put in writing before moving in.
